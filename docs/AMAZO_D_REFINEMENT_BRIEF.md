# Brief de refinamento pendente — Amazô D

## Estado atual

A **Opção D — Rio Neon** está provisoriamente aplicada como imagem principal da Amazô na LP. A Lídi aprovou os dreads, o sorriso largo, os olhos levemente puxados, o tom de pele, os sinais naturais e a botânica fora do rosto. O refinamento pendente deve reduzir a aparência pictórica/anime e retomar o caráter digital-editorial da Amazô anterior.

## Direção de arte para a próxima geração

> Edit the approved Amazô option D. Preserve long forest-toned dreadlocks, warm medium-brown skin, subtly almond-shaped dark eyes, broad confident smile, small gold hoop nose ring, fine vertical line only under the lower lip, and natural beauty marks. Keep the face completely free of leaves, vines, water marks, botanical tattoos, neon lines, glyphs and face paint. Transform the rendering into a premium digital-editorial illustration: controlled vector-like contours, layered translucent digital surfaces, crisp ink edges, restrained halftone and interface-grid texture, with a contemporary Brazilian Amazonian creative-tech language. Make the neon water currents appear as cyan-teal and muted-magenta river-data flows around the dreadlocks and shoulders, never crossing the face. Keep botanical forms on neck, shoulders, arms, clothing and background only. Use the fern palette: #102B27, #648D3C, #391E13, #D7F993 accents, #D9AAA1 and #3E2128. Avoid anime, painterly realism, photorealism, generic cyberpunk, smooth loose hair, face ornaments, distorted anatomy and extra fingers. No text, logo or watermark.
